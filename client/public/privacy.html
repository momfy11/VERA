Privacy Policy

Application: VERA – Voice-Enabled Reasoning Assistant
Owner: Srecko Radivojevic
Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VERA collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application. VERA is a private, personal-use application and is not publicly distributed.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google OAuth, VERA receives the following information from Google:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your display name
  • An OAuth access token used to access Google Calendar and Gmail on your behalf

During use of the application, VERA may store:

  • Conversation history within your session
  • User preferences and facts you share, stored as memory entries to personalize responses
  • Audit logs of tool actions performed on your behalf (e.g. emails sent, calendar events created)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To access Google Calendar and Gmail on your behalf, solely based on your instructions
  • To personalise responses by recalling stored preferences across sessions
  • To log tool actions for traceability and safety purposes

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Google API Services

VERA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are only used to perform actions you explicitly request.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in a private PostgreSQL database hosted on a dedicated server. Access is restricted to the application and its owner. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. No data is stored in third-party analytics or tracking services.

5. Data Retention

Conversation history and memory entries are retained until you explicitly clear them or your account is deleted. You may request deletion of all stored data by contacting the owner.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Request a copy of all data stored about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Revoke Google access via your Google Account permissions

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application owner at: momfy86@gmail.com

This policy applies solely to the VERA application and does not cover any third-party services accessed through it.
